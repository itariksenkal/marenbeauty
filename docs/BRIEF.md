# BRIEF — Maren Beauty

Business, audience, positioning and tone of voice. Written in English; every
quoted example of site copy is Turkish, because that is what ships.

---

## 1. The business

**Maren Beauty** is a boutique beauty centre (güzellik merkezi) in
**Konya / Selçuklu**, opening soon. It offers twenty skin, hair-removal and
brow/lash services delivered one client at a time in a quiet, warm, private
space.

|                         |                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brand name              | Maren Beauty                                                  |
| Category                | Güzellik merkezi — **not** a medical clinic, not a polyclinic |
| Location shown publicly | Konya, Selçuklu                                               |
| Street address          | Deliberately not published — see `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`     |
| Domain                  | marenbeauty.com                                               |
| Status                  | **Pre-launch.** Not yet open, no operating history            |
| Legal entity            | Undecided — `{{LEGAL_ENTITY}}` until confirmed                |
| Team                    | One-to-one service; team not yet named publicly               |
| Price positioning       | Premium. **No prices published anywhere**, not even ranges    |

### What "boutique" means here, operationally

- One client at a time. No waiting room churn, no conveyor belt.
- Appointment-led. There is no booking system yet; the conversion path is a
  message, not a checkout.
- The room, the light and the pace are part of the product. The site has to
  carry that feeling before anyone has seen the space.

## 2. The name

**Maren** relates to the sea (_mare_). This is not decoration — it is the
organising idea for the entire site.

The sea gives us the register the brand needs and the medical register it must
avoid: **calm, continuous, unhurried, clear, restorative.** Water does not
promise. It settles.

This reads through into:

- **Motion** — one continuous water-surface form that transforms down the page:
  still at the hero, spreading through the brand story, dispersing into light
  behind the services, settling again at the closing call to action. Full spec
  in `docs/MOTION.md`.
- **Colour** — warm cream, nude, rose beige and ivory: sand and light, not
  cold blue. The sea reference is in the _behaviour_, not the palette. A blue
  beauty site would read clinical, which is precisely wrong.
- **Copy rhythm** — short sentences. Space between ideas. Nothing shouted.

**Never** literalise this. No wave illustrations, no nautical motifs, no shells,
no anchors, no blue gradients, no "dalga" metaphors in body copy. The water is
felt in movement and pacing, never depicted.

## 3. Audience

### Primary — "the returning regular"

Women, roughly 25–45, living in Selçuklu and central Konya. Professionals,
often with children. They already spend on personal care and have used at least
one beauty centre before. They are not price-shopping; they are quality- and
trust-shopping, usually after a disappointment somewhere else.

**What they want to know, in order:**

1. Is this place clean, private and calm?
2. Does the person applying this know what they are doing?
3. What actually happens during the session — how long, how it feels?
4. Is it near me?
5. How do I ask a question without committing to anything?

**What makes them leave:** loud design, aggressive discount banners, stock
photos that obviously are not the venue, walls of exclamation marks,
before/after collages, a phone number that does not work.

### Secondary — "the occasion client"

Brides, bridal parties, mothers of the couple, graduates. Highly seasonal,
high value, plan weeks ahead, research thoroughly, decide as a group. They
arrive on the bridal package page from search and want reassurance and a
timeline, not a hard sell.

### Tertiary — "the researcher"

Someone searching a question, not a business: _lazer epilasyon nedir_,
_kirpik lifting ne kadar kalıcı_, _karma cilt nasıl anlaşılır_. They may not
convert this visit. The blog exists for them: answer the question completely,
without a sales pitch, and be the place they remember when they are ready.

### Explicitly not the audience

People looking for medical procedures, injectables, or anything requiring a
physician. The site should not attract them, and must never imply it serves
them.

## 4. Positioning

> **Maren Beauty is the quiet, careful alternative to the busy salon.**
> One person, one room, full attention — for women in Konya who have learned
> that the difference is in the care, not the equipment list.

### Positioning axes

| Dimension | Maren sits at              | Not                             |
| --------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Pace      | Unhurried, appointment-led | Walk-in, high-volume            |
| Register  | Editorial, restrained      | Promotional, discount-led       |
| Authority | Craft and consistency      | Clinical / medical authority    |
| Proof     | Transparency about process | Results photography, statistics |
| Scale     | Boutique, personal         | Chain, franchise                |

### Competitive reality in Konya

The local category is crowded and mostly competes on price and volume:
Instagram-first, discount codes, before/after grids, dense pages of claims.
A restrained, well-written, fast, honest website is genuinely differentiating
in this market — and it is the only lever available before the doors open.

### Credibility while pre-launch

There is no operating history, no reviews, no photographs of the real space,
no Google Business Profile. Credibility therefore has to come from the only
assets that exist today:

1. **Precision.** Describing each service accurately and completely signals
   competence more strongly than any adjective.
2. **Restraint.** Saying less than competitors, and saying it better, reads as
   confidence.
3. **Honesty about status.** "Yakında açılıyoruz" stated plainly builds more
   trust than pretending to be open. Do not fake a history.
4. **Craft of the site itself.** Speed, accessibility and typography are read
   as care, even by visitors who could not name why.

Testimonials ship as an empty array and stay empty until real ones exist.

## 5. Tone of voice

### The voice in one line

**A calm expert who has nothing to prove.**

### Principles

**1. Describe, do not promise.**
Say what happens in the room, how long it takes, how it feels, what to expect
afterwards. Let accuracy do the persuading.

> ✅ "Uygulama yaklaşık 45 dakika sürer. Cildiniz temizlendikten sonra
> nemlendirici bir maske uygulanır."
> ❌ "Cildinizi yeniler ve gençleştirir!"

**2. Short sentences. Real punctuation.**
Aim for 12–18 words. One idea per sentence. No exclamation marks anywhere on
the site — not one.

**3. Second person plural, warm but not familiar.**
`siz` throughout. Never `sen`. Never "canım", "güzelim", "tatlım". Address an
adult who is deciding how to spend her money.

**4. Concrete over abstract.**
"Seans sonrası ciltte hafif bir kızarıklık görülebilir; genellikle kısa sürede
geçer" beats "mükemmel sonuçlar".

**5. No superlatives, no urgency.**
No "en iyi", "Konya'nın 1 numaralı", "kaçırmayın", "son fırsat", "hemen".
Nothing that manufactures pressure.

**6. Appearance language, never treatment language.**
This is a legal constraint as well as a stylistic one — see `CLAUDE.md` §9.

> ✅ "…görünümünü iyileştirmeye yardımcı olur"
> ✅ "…destekler", "…hedefler", "…amaçlanır"
> ❌ "…tedavi eder", "…iyileştirir", "…yok eder", "…garanti"

**7. Answer the question the visitor actually asked.**
Especially in the blog. Get to the answer in the first paragraph. Do not open
with three paragraphs of throat-clearing about how important skin care is.

**8. Admit limits.**
"Her cilt farklıdır" and "uygunluk seans öncesi değerlendirilir" are strengths.
They are honest, they are legally safer, and they sound like an expert rather
than a brochure.

### Vocabulary

**Use:** bakım, uygulama, seans, rutin, destekler, görünüm, canlılık, nem
dengesi, cilt bariyeri, hazırlık, sonrası bakım, uygunluk değerlendirmesi,
kişiye özel, sakin, özenli.

**Never use:** tedavi, terapi, kür, iyileştirir, yok eder, garanti, kesin
sonuç, mucize, %-based efficacy claims, "klinik", "doktor kontrolünde",
"tıbbi".

Note "klinik" and "tıbbi": these are not on the build-blocking list, but using
them would position Maren as something it is not licensed to be. Avoid them.

### Register by page type

| Page           | Register                            | Length              |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Home           | Editorial, atmospheric, few words   | Very short          |
| Service detail | Informative, procedural, reassuring | 500–800 words       |
| Blog post      | Helpful, plain, complete            | 900–1400 words      |
| Legal          | Formal, precise, unstyled           | As long as required |
| Forms & errors | Plain, human, never cute            | One line            |

## 6. Conversion model

There is no booking system and no phone number yet. The conversion hierarchy is
designed around **WhatsApp** as the eventual primary channel, degrading
gracefully to the contact form today.

```
1. WhatsApp        ← primary once configured; lowest friction, matches local habit
2. Phone call      ← secondary once configured
3. Contact form    ← always available; the only live channel today
4. Instagram       ← discovery / atmosphere, not a conversion channel
```

Rules:

- Any unconfigured channel renders **nothing at all** — see `CLAUDE.md` §7.
  A dead link is worse than an absent one.
- The contact form is never buried. It is reachable from every page.
- One primary action per screen. Never two competing buttons of equal weight.
- No pop-ups, no exit-intent overlays, no newsletter interstitial, no chat
  widget.

---

## 7. Success, before there is traffic

Pre-launch, the site cannot be judged on conversions. It is judged on:

1. Every one of the 20 services has a complete, accurate, compliant page.
2. Zero medical or efficacy claims survive `npm run guard`.
3. WCAG AA across every route, verified — not estimated.
4. The site is fully self-hostable and portable off Vercel.
5. Content is structured so the owner can publish a post herself later without
   a developer.
6. Every unknown is written down in `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` rather than
   invented.

Post-launch metrics belong in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, after the Google Business
Profile exists and there is something real to measure.
